FAQ

Collapsible content sample for typography hierarchy, borders, and hover states.

How do palette roles stay consistent across pages?

Semantic roles are mapped first, then swatches are assigned. Text, controls, and focus tokens are checked for visibility before apply.

Can we lock backgrounds but vary accent families?

Yes. Keep mode fixed and shuffle tone layers only. This preserves readability while exploring brand expression.

What if a palette has too many similar neutrals?

The system can generate missing support tones from contrast-safe mixes, then validate hierarchy and interaction clarity.
